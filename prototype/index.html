The Train Game

Prototype 0.6 · Advanced-steam west

Ride the rail as far as you can. The conductor drives and keeps her fed; you keep the riders off. Every arrow you loose is gone unless you pull it back out of wherever it stuck. A hard hit up top can throw you off the train: get your hook out, fast.

MoveLeft stick (WASD on desktop). Double-tap forward and hold to sprint. Walk the roof, take the ladder at the back-right corner down to the rear platform, step through the door to get inside. CrouchCrouch / hold Shift. Duck below the roof boards and the cabin windows. Hits shove you around far less. LookDrag anywhere on the view (mouse on desktop; click the view to capture it). While holding Draw, that finger aims. ShootHold Draw (or hold the left mouse button): a one-second draw on the Minecraft curve. A snap shot leaves at 100 mph, a full draw at 180. The arrow leaves the bow in your hand and meets the cross at 15–35 m. Release to loose, and draw again straight away. AnatomyEvery part of horse and rider is its own target: a hit breaks that joint and the part flies off with whatever hangs from it. SurgeSurge / Q, from the tender only: three charges a stretch, each one stacks on the last, up to 200 mph, then she settles back to cruise. Towns restock the steam. Your call, never the conductor's. InteractRight click, Grab or E: pull out a stuck arrow you're looking at (or tap it), talk to the conductor from the front of the tender, climb aboard a stopped train. JumpJump / Space. ItemsMouse wheel or 1 / 2 switches bow and grappling hook. HookKnocked off? Scroll to the hook and fire it at the train. If it bites, it reels you back aboard. Miss, and the train won't stop: walk up the line to where the conductor waits for you. StatsHold Tab (or the Stats button) for the full readout. Speed stays on; tap it to swap mph and km/h. HatsThe hardest shot: clip a rider's hat and it spins off. The first hat of each gang boomerangs onto the rack in the hat car, arrow and all. Look at a racked hat and press Grab to wear it; Grab again pulls the arrow out.
