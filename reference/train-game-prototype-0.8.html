The Train Game

Prototype 0.8 · Steampunk cyborg west

Ride the rail as far as you can. The conductor drives and keeps her fed; you keep the riders off. Every arrow you loose is gone unless you pull it back out of wherever it stuck. A hard hit up top can throw you off the train: fire your hand at it, fast. You're a machine from the wrists down: your hands float on magnets, and one of them flies.

MoveLeft stick (WASD on desktop). Double-tap forward and hold to sprint. Walk the roof, take the ladder at the back-right corner down to the rear platform, step through the door to get inside. CrouchCrouch / hold Shift. Duck below the roof boards and the cabin windows. Hits shove you around far less. LookDrag anywhere on the view (mouse on desktop; click the view to capture it). While holding Draw, that finger aims. ShootHold Draw (or hold the left mouse button): a one-second draw on the Minecraft curve. A snap shot leaves at 100 mph, a full draw at 180. The arrow leaves the bow in your hand and meets the cross at 15–35 m. Release to loose, and draw again straight away. AnatomyEvery part of horse and rider is its own target: a hit breaks that joint and the part flies off with whatever hangs from it. SurgeSurge / Q, from the tender only: three charges a stretch, each one stacks on the last, up to 200 mph, then she settles back to cruise. Towns restock the steam. Your call, never the conductor's. HandRight click, Grab or E fires your right hand out on its electric tether. Tap: a quick grab up to 7 m. Hold: charge it, up to 26 m; let go to fire. It takes stuck arrows (outside the cars too), arrows on the ground and riders' hats. At full stretch it hangs open for a moment, catching whatever drifts in, then snaps back. Tap Draw to cancel a charge; tap Grab to cancel a draw. Close up, a tap also wears a racked hat, talks to the conductor and climbs a stopped train. JumpJump / Space. ItemsMouse wheel or 1 / 2: the bow, or both hands free. Thrown offHold Grab, aim at any car and let go. The hand clamps on and you're dragged along behind, reeling yourself in. Miss, and the train won't stop: walk up the line to where the conductor waits for you. StatsHold Tab (or the Stats button) for the full readout. Speed stays on; tap it to swap mph and km/h. HatsThe hardest shot: clip a rider's hat and it spins off. The first hat of each gang boomerangs onto the rack in the hat car, arrow and all. Look at a racked hat and press Grab to wear it; Grab again pulls the arrow out.
