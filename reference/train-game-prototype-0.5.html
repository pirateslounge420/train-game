The Train Game

Prototype 0.5 · Advanced-steam west

Ride the rail as far as you can. Riders come at the train with bows. Every arrow you loose is gone unless you pull it back out of wherever it stuck: the roof, the walls inside, the back platform.

MoveLeft stick (WASD on desktop). Walk the roof, take the ladder at the back-right corner down to the rear platform, step through the door to get inside. LookDrag anywhere on the view (mouse on desktop; click the view to capture it). While holding Draw, that finger aims. ShootHold Draw (or the left mouse button). A snap shot leaves at 100 mph, a full draw at 180. The sight is zeroed, so inside 35 m the arrow lands on the cross. Release to loose, and draw again straight away. GrabWalk up to a stuck arrow and tap it, or look at it and press Grab / E. JumpJump / Space. SurgeSurge / Shift: a stored-steam burst. Riders fall behind. Refills slowly. ItemsMouse wheel or 1 / 2 switches bow and shovel. Shovel coal in the tender to build steam. StatsHold Tab (or the Stats button) for the full readout. Speed stays on; tap it to swap mph and km/h. HatsThe hardest shot: clip a rider's hat and it spins off. The first hat of each gang boomerangs onto the rack in the hat car.
